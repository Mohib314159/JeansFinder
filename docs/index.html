How it works, and what's next

How it works

  • Collect. A scraper runs my saved Vinted searches every 45–90 minutes and pulls new listings, skipping anything already seen or outside my size.
  • Filter, then rank. A cheap colour check drops obvious misses before the slow model runs. What's left goes through CLIP: each photo becomes 512 numbers, and the score is the average similarity to my three closest references, minus a penalty for looking like something I disliked.
  • Learn. A like crops the photo to the jeans and adds it as a reference; score ranges recalibrate from the references themselves.
  • Here vs there. On this page all of that runs in your browser on a saved snapshot. The real app runs on my PC and I check the feed on my phone.

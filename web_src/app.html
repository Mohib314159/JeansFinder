JeansFinder

I use this to find one specific style of jeans on Vinted: baggy, dark grey, early-2000s. It checks new listings automatically, throws out obviously wrong colours, then ranks the rest by how much the photos look like examples I like. Try it: like or dislike a few listings and watch the feed re-rank.

Filtered out before CLIP

A cheap colour check runs first. It averages the pixels in the middle of the photo (skipping white backgrounds) and throws out anything close to a colour I never want. This skips the slow image model for most listings.

Collect

A scraper runs my saved Vinted searches every 45 to 90 minutes and pulls new listings with their photos. It skips listings it has already seen and anything outside my size.

Filter, then rank

The colour check removes obvious misses. Everything else goes through CLIP, which turns each photo into a 512-number vector. The score is the average similarity to my 3 closest references, minus a penalty if it looks like something I disliked.

Learn

A like crops the photo to the jeans and adds it as a reference, and score cut-offs recalibrate from the references themselves. On this page all of that runs in your browser on a real snapshot; the real app does it on my PC and sends matches to my phone.
